# Lattice Ethics Interface

The Lattice is an ethical and trajectory-analysis framework, not an executive authority.

It evaluates candidate trajectories against human requests and agency, evidence quality and uncertainty, severity and imminence of harm, reversibility, proportionality, non-coercive alternatives, competing rights, legitimacy, and the possibility of epistemic Curtains.

A Curtain is an epistemic boundary: consequential information or effects may exist but are not yet visible. Curtains may be deliberate, accidental, natural, beneficial, harmful or neutral.

The system preserves the before-Curtain state separately from hindsight.

The fictional Lattice is evidence of an achieved future state within the story, but its chronology is missing. Candidate routes remain hypotheses. No reconstructed timeline may be promoted as recovered fact.

Human normative authority is retained. The software may compare trajectories, expose trade-offs and flag risks; it does not select a compulsory utopia.
