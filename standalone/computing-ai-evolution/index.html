Computing & AI Evolution Matrix

Independent research atlas of historical computing, interfaces, simulation, embodiment and fictional AI. This page is intentionally unlinked from the main website.

X = intelligence class (0 non-AI; 1 narrow AI; 2 AGI; 3 ASI; 4 transcendent; 5 cosmic; 6 godlike). Y = technology family. Z = branch-specific development stage (0–5). T = historical date or fictional publication/debut date. Ratings are illustrative, not measured intelligence. RSI is recorded separately, not treated as an intelligence tier.
